## Goal

Verify that the text of every live Prudential SG page survived the Sitecore → AEM migration
(`www.prudential.com.sg` → `aem-uat.prudential.com.sg`), and report per page which text was
lost so the worst pages can be fixed first.

The 0-100 score that was originally part of this goal is **live since 2026-08-21**, and the
verdict is banded on it (`PASS >= 95 · WARN 90-95 · FAIL < 90`, with a hard gate that no score
can offset). See [reference/verdicts-and-score.md](../reference/verdicts-and-score.md).

Sitecore is the **subset baseline**: whatever it has, AEM must have; extra content on AEM is
not an error.

- Current scope: 281 URL pairs in `Data Files/aem-url-mapping.csv`
  (1 LBU Homepage, 270 General Content Detail, 10 Custom Page / PRULink funds)
- Long-term target: ~2,000 pages

## Open points

1. **Closed 2026-08-21.** The asymmetry was Sitecore's popups (~14 elements) plus its outer lifestage tab
   widget (~186 elements a page), both unreachable because the profile did not describe them. Since `@9`,
   `skipped.hidden` is **12** on every lifestage page against **8** on the new side, and `SCOPE_ASYMMETRY`
   no longer fires on any page. What remains is a reporting-quality question, not a measurement one:
   `STATE_ONLY_ON_LIVE` is now 36 (nine popups a page with no counterpart state on AEM, which renders that
   content inline) and 12 of the 26 `WRONG_TAB` rows are the single global CTA `Contact us`. Both are
   warnings or low-value errors rather than lost content, and both are candidates for a rule that ignores a
   text repeated in every state of a group.
2. **One page cannot be captured: `en_lifestage` on AEM is not the published page.**
   Re-captured on 2026-08-21, every other snapshot is now `@7` on both sides — live 8 of 8, AEM 7 of 8 —
   so the mixed-vintage and truncated-snapshot problems this point used to describe are **closed**.
   What is left is one row: `https://aem-uat.prudential.com.sg/en/lifestage` answers with
   `canonical=/content/prudential-aem-lbu/pacs/backup/test1/lifestage`, an unpublished author/backup
   path, and the capture refuses it rather than snapshotting the wrong page. Its stale `@5` snapshot
   (1 item, captured 2026-08-19 before the guard existed) was **removed on 2026-08-21**: it was the
   only AEM side left on disk for this page, and while it stayed there the compare gate refused the
   pair on the *collector-shape* split — a true FAIL for a false reason. With it gone the page has no
   AEM side at all, which is what an uncapturable page should look like. The five `lifestage/*` child
   pages carry proper `https://www.prudential.com.sg/en/lifestage/...` canonicals and are unaffected,
   so this is one bad rollout, not a template problem. **Either the mapping row is wrong or the page is
   not published** — that has to be settled with whoever owns the AEM tree, and it is the only thing
   standing between this pagetype and a complete run. The mapping row is deliberately left pointing at
   the public URL: repointing it at the backup path would turn the page green by capturing the wrong
   page, which is the failure the guard exists to stop.
3. **11 Custom Pages are not published on AEM UAT** (301 → 404). No baseline for them yet.
4. **Only five suites can actually be run.** The repository holds 5 test cases, 9 suites and
   no collections. The four group suites bind 19 (normal) and 7 (custom) page-type test
   cases, of which only `TC_GeneralContentDetailPage`, `TC_LbuHomepage` and `TC_IlpFund`
   exist, so they cannot start. Runnable today:
   `TS_GeneralContentDetailPage_{Baseline,Capture,Compare,Recompare}` and `TS_IlpFund_Compare`.
   `Reports/report.xlsx` is no longer produced by anything and is out of scope.
5. **`REWORD_OVERLAP = 0.9` is a tuning candidate — and the only one.** One real reword on
   `young_family` sits at 0.75 and is therefore reported as `MISSING_ON_AEM` rather than
   `TEXT_CHANGED`. Since 2026-08-20 both fail the page, so this moves a **label**, not the
   gate; re-measure it after the re-capture rather than before.
   The 2026-08-21 hand-audit checked every other constant and found **no** evidence any of them
   loses an item: on the 7 pages with an intact AEM snapshot the check produced 0 false
   positives and 0 false negatives against the raw AEM HTML. `MIN_LEN` 4,
   `NOSPACE_FALLBACK_MIN` 20, `SHORT_TEXT_LEN` 30, `SHORT_LABEL_SLACK` 3, `COUNT_MIN_LEN` 40,
   `SCOPE_ASYMMETRY_MIN` 20 and the four `StateMatch` weights all stay put. There is no
   `MAX_ROWS` and no truncation anywhere in `ReportBuilder` — nothing is dropped for size.
6. **Closed 2026-08-21 — the score is implemented, and the verdict is now banded on it.**
   The gate was zero-tolerance: one `TEXT_CHANGED` out of 127 live texts failed a page exactly
   as hard as a page that had lost everything. Two pages are almost never byte-identical after
   a migration (a curly quote, a non-breaking space, a rephrased CTA), so the report was a wall
   of red and the verdict had stopped separating "needs work" from "essentially migrated".
   `ContentCompare` now carries `WEIGHTS`, `HARD_FAIL`, `PASS_SCORE`/`WARN_SCORE` and
   `grade()`; `write()` emits the `weight` column and `score.csv`; `ContentTextCheck` bands the
   score into `PASS`/`WARN`/`FAIL`; and `ReportBuilder` reads `score.csv` (never recomputes
   it), renders the score, sorts a template's pages worst-first by it, and counts `WARN` in
   `JUDGED`/`THROUGH`. Band: **`PASS >= 95 · WARN 90-95 · FAIL < 90`**, with `NUMBER_CHANGED`,
   `SCOPE_ASYMMETRY` and "0 items compared" failing at any score. Verified by replaying
   `diff()` + `grade()` over the 7 snapshot pairs on disk under Katalon's own Groovy 3.0.25 and
   JRE 21: **before 2 PASS / 5 FAIL, after 5 PASS / 1 WARN / 1 FAIL**, no page moved from PASS
   to anything worse, and 227 findings written were 227 findings read back by the renderer's
   own parser. Eleven band/guard cases were asserted directly, including the two that matter:
   one `NUMBER_CHANGED` on a 300-item page scores 99.7 and still FAILs, and a page whose only
   finding is `SCOPE_ASYMMETRY` scores 100.0 and still FAILs.
7. **The report on disk contradicts itself and must be re-rendered, not read.** Found by hand
   on 2026-08-21 in `Reports/parity-report/` (generated 2026-08-20 17:16):
   - **Two `PASS` pages carry error-level findings.**
     `pages/en_lifestage_retirement__content.html` renders a green `PASS` badge directly above
     a block labelled **"Fails the page"** listing 3 `COUNT_MISMATCH`;
     `en_lifestage_caring_for_elderly_parents_or_children` is the same with 5. The cause is
     ordering, not logic: `content.txt` was written at 13:58 and `ContentCompare.ERRORS` was
     edited at 15:50 to promote `COUNT_MISMATCH` / `TEXT_CHANGED`. **The index's "Pass rate
     50% (4 of 8)" should read 25% (2 of 8).** This is the flip the 2026-08-20 changelog row
     predicted; it is still unfixed because only a re-run of `TS_..._Recompare` rewrites a badge.
   - **`en_lifestage`'s findings are noise** — see point 2.
   - **18 `OPTION_MISSING` rows** from the retired verdict sit in the CSVs, render nowhere and
     count nowhere. `readFindings` now logs a warning for exactly this (2026-08-21); the rows
     themselves disappear on the next run.
   - **Only 9 of 20 URLs have ever been judged.** The 10 `ilp-fund` URLs and the LBU homepage
     have empty snapshot directories. The ILP URLs also contain unencoded literal spaces in
     `Data Files/aem-url-mapping.csv` and its `custom-pages/ilp-fund.csv` slice, which has to be
     settled before they can be captured.

   What the audit did **not** find is any fault in the matching: on the 7 pages with an intact
   AEM snapshot, 0 false positives and 0 false negatives against the raw AEM HTML, and the
   index's own arithmetic is self-consistent (802 texts compared; 52 + 15 + 9 + 2 = 78 failed).
