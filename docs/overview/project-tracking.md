## Goal

Verify that the text of every live Prudential SG page survived the Sitecore → AEM migration
(`www.prudential.com.sg` → `aem-uat.prudential.com.sg`), and report per page which text was
lost so the worst pages can be fixed first.

The 0-100 score that was originally part of this goal is **deferred**: the report under
construction is verdict-only (open point 6).

Sitecore is the **subset baseline**: whatever it has, AEM must have; extra content on AEM is
not an error.

- Current scope: 20 URL pairs in `Data Files/aem-url-mapping.csv`
  (1 LBU Homepage, 9 General Content Detail, 10 Custom Page / PRULink funds)
- Long-term target: ~2,000 pages

## Open points

1. **Every score on the carried data is low confidence.** Six pages fire `SCOPE_ASYMMETRY`
   (the live side discards 83–160 hidden elements against 8 on the new side) and three have
   fewer than 40 items. The report withholds the site average and says why rather than
   printing a figure that would have to be un-learned. Fixing this is a capture-side job.
2. **Every snapshot on disk has to be re-captured, `@4` and `@5` alike.** The `@4` ones were
   taken before the capture-side fixes (CSS background heroes, inline `<svg>`, opening hidden
   content before collection, `scrollFullPage` measuring `documentElement`). The `@5` ones —
   all eight `.sitecore.json` files and `en_lifestage.aem.json` — are **truncated**: the CTA
   `href` block shadowed the item loop's counter and ended the crawl at the first link on the
   page (fixed 2026-08-20, see the changelog), so they hold 1–5 items each. Any comparison
   run over them, and every finding read out of one, is void. A fresh baseline + capture at
   `@5` with the fixed collector is what makes the numbers final.
   *Note also that open point 1 is measured on the `@4` files and will have to be re-read
   after the re-capture.*
3. **11 Custom Pages are not published on AEM UAT** (301 → 404). No baseline for them yet.
4. **Only five suites can actually be run.** The repository holds 5 test cases, 9 suites and
   no collections. The four group suites bind 19 (normal) and 7 (custom) page-type test
   cases, of which only `TC_GeneralContentDetailPage`, `TC_LbuHomepage` and `TC_IlpFund`
   exist, so they cannot start. Runnable today:
   `TS_GeneralContentDetailPage_{Baseline,Capture,Compare,Recompare}` and `TS_IlpFund_Compare`.
   `Reports/report.xlsx` is no longer produced by anything and is out of scope.
5. **`REWORD_OVERLAP = 0.9` is a tuning candidate.** One real reword on `young_family` sits
   at 0.75 and is therefore reported as an error-level `MISSING_ON_AEM` rather than a
   warning-level `TEXT_CHANGED`.
6. **The score is deferred by decision, and absent from the committed code.** As of
   2026-08-20 the report being built is **verdict-only** (no score, no screenshots) — see
   [reference/content-result-contract.md](../reference/content-result-contract.md) — so
   nothing consumes a score today and restoring it is no longer on the critical path. The
   code state below is unchanged and still what makes `run.sh` red; the two offline harnesses
   assert a score the keywords do not produce, and that has to be settled either by restoring
   `score()` or by retiring those assertions.
   `Keywords/migration/checks/ContentCompare.groovy` (434 lines) has no `WEIGHTS` and no
   `score()`; `write()` emits `findings.csv` with header `verdict,kind,path,text,note` — no
   `weight` column — and never writes `score.csv`; `summary()` appends no score clause; and
   `ReportBuilder` renders no score chip or panel. `tools/offline-checks/verdict-rules.groovy`
   and `report-contract.groovy` both call `ContentCompare.score(...)`, so `run.sh` cannot pass
   in this state — which means the 🟢 rows in the status table above describe the earlier build
   that produced the files still sitting in `Reports/` (their `findings.csv` does carry a
   `weight` column and a `score.csv` exists). Either the scoring half was lost before the first
   commit or it was never committed; restoring it against
   [reference/verdicts-and-score.md](../reference/verdicts-and-score.md) is a separate job, and
   the assertions are the specification to restore it to. **Not caused by the workflow-doc
   change** — recorded here because writing the diagram surfaced it.
