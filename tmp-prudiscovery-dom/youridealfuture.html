Did You Know...

If you are using an HDB Concessionary Loan, you will need to make a downpayment of 10% of the purchase price or valuation, whichever is higher. The downpayment can be paid from your CPF Ordinary Account (OA), in cash, or througha combination of both. If you qualify for HDB’s Staggered Downpayment Scheme, this downpayment can be split into two installments: 5% to be paid during signing of agreement and another 5% when collecting your keys.

If you are using a private bank loan, you will need to make a downpayment of 25% of the purchase price or valuation, whichever is higher. At least 5% of this downpayment must be in cash and the remaining 20% can be paid with the monies of your CPF OA, in cash, or a combination of both. Note that you must use a private bank loan for ECs.
